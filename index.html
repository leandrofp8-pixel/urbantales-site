FAQ

Before you walk

Does Urban Tales work in my city?

Yes. Urban Tales finds the landmarks around you in any city, town or village and tells their stories. The city guides on this site are our most researched places, not the limit.

How much does Urban Tales cost?

The app is free to download and includes 3 free landmarks. A Day Pass ($2.99) unlocks every city for 24 hours, a City Unlock ($5.99) keeps one city unlocked forever, and Unlimited costs $29.99 a year.

Do I need to book or join a group?

No. Download the app, open the map and walk. There's no booking, no group and no fixed schedule.

What languages does Urban Tales support?

English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French and German.

Does it work without internet?

No. Stories and maps load as you walk, so you need mobile data. A local SIM or a travel eSIM works well abroad.

How is it different from other audio guide apps?

Most sell pre-made tours for a list of cities. Urban Tales tells the story of whatever landmark you're near, in any city. We compared six apps, including where others are the better pick, in our audio guide app comparison.
