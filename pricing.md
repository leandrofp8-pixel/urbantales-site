# Urban Tales pricing

Urban Tales is a GPS audio guide app for iPhone and Android (https://urbantales.net). It is free to download.
Prices are in US dollars; the App Store and Google Play show the local price in each country. Last checked: 2026-11-08.

## Free
- Price: $0
- 3 free stories, in any city.
- Includes the live map of nearby landmarks and all 5 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German).
- Trip planner: free. Pick a city, dates and pace; every day is ordered with opening hours, closing days, a lunch break and a tip per stop. Shared trips can be followed by friends on their own dates.
- Web trip planner: one free plan per visitor at https://urbantales.net/plan.html, then continue in the app.

## Day Pass
- Price: 500 coins (a 500-coin pack is $2.99)
- Unlimited stories everywhere for 24 hours.

## City Unlock
- Price: 1,000 coins (a 1,000-coin pack is $5.99)
- Unlimited stories in one city, with no expiry.

## Unlimited (annual)
- Price: $29.99 a year, auto-renewing subscription.
- Unlimited stories in every city.

## Coin packs (one-time purchases)
- 500 coins: $2.99
- 1,000 coins: $5.99
- 2,000 coins: $9.99
- 5,000 coins: $19.99

No subscription is needed for the Day Pass or City Unlock.

Download: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760130598 · https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.urbantales.app

## Included with paid access
- Pocket Mode plays landmark stories with your phone locked.
- Download tours and their audio beforehand to listen offline, within your plan’s places and validity period.
- Connect to discover additional landmarks or refresh practical information.
