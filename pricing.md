# Urban Tales pricing

Urban Tales is a GPS audio guide app for iPhone and Android (https://urbantales.net). It is free to download.
Prices are in US dollars; the App Store and Google Play show the local price in each country. Last checked: 2026-10-22.

## Free
- Price: $0
- 3 free stories a day, in any city.
- Includes the live map of nearby landmarks and all 5 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German).

## Day Pass
- Price: 500 coins (a 500-coin pack is $2.99)
- Unlimited stories everywhere for 24 hours.

## City Unlock
- Price: 1,000 coins (a 1,000-coin pack is $5.99)
- Unlimited stories in one city, with no expiry.

## Unlimited (annual)
- Price: $29.99 a year, auto-renewing subscription.
- Unlimited stories in every city.

## Coin packs (one-time purchases)
- 500 coins: $2.99
- 1,000 coins: $5.99
- 2,000 coins: $9.99
- 5,000 coins: $19.99

No subscription is needed for the Day Pass or City Unlock.
