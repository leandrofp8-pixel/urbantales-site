Three stories most visitors miss

1

Three men, one statue

Brazilian engineer Heitor da Silva Costa designed it, French sculptor Paul Landowski modelled the head and hands in Paris, and Romanian sculptor Gheorghe Leonida carved the face.

2

A skin of soapstone

The concrete is covered with thousands of small triangles of soapstone, chosen because it's durable and easy to work.

3

Lightning on the summit

As the highest point around, the statue is struck by lightning several times a year; in 2014 a strike chipped the tip of the right thumb.

Christ the Redeemer: common questions

When was Christ the Redeemer built?

Construction took about nine years; the statue was inaugurated in 1931.

Who made Christ the Redeemer?

Engineer Heitor da Silva Costa designed it, Paul Landowski sculpted the head and hands, and Gheorghe Leonida carved the face.

How tall is Christ the Redeemer?

The statue is 30 metres tall, on an 8-metre pedestal, about 700 metres above sea level.
