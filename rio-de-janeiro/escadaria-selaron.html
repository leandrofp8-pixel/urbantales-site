Three stories most visitors miss

1

It began at his front door

In 1990 Selarón started covering the broken steps outside his house; the project grew until it reached the top.

2

Tiles from everywhere

He paid for it by selling his paintings, and visitors from all over the world sent or brought tiles, so you can find pieces from more than 60 countries.

3

Green, yellow and blue

The main colours are those of the Brazilian flag, a homage to the country that took him in; look for the pregnant woman he painted on hundreds of tiles himself.

TipArrive early in the morning to photograph the steps without the crowds, and look for tiles from your own country. With Urban Tales running, the steps' story plays as you reach the bottom in Lapa.

Escadaria Selarón: common questions

Who made the Escadaria Selarón?

Chilean-born artist Jorge Selarón, who started in 1990 and kept changing tiles until his death in 2013.

How many steps does the Escadaria Selarón have?

215 steps, about 125 metres, between Rua Joaquim Silva in Lapa and Rua Pinto Martins in Santa Teresa.

Where do the Selarón tiles come from?

More than 60 countries; many were sent or brought by visitors.
