Three stories most visitors miss

1

Water from the hills

The aqueduct brought water from the Carioca River in the Santa Teresa hills to the fountains of central Rio. The arches were finished in 1750, replacing a first aqueduct of 1723.

2

From water to trams

When the city's water supply was modernised, the arches became a viaduct: since 1896 the Santa Teresa tram has crossed them.

3

Lapa after dark

The neighbourhood under the arches became Rio's best-known nightlife district, a historic home of samba and choro.

Arcos da Lapa: common questions

How old are the Arcos da Lapa?

The arches were completed in 1750, replacing an earlier aqueduct finished in 1723.

Can you ride over the Arcos da Lapa?

Yes, the Santa Teresa tram crosses the arches on its way up the hill.

How long are the Arcos da Lapa?

About 270 metres, with 42 arches.
