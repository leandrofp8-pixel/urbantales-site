Santa Maria del Mar: built by the dockworkers

Begun in 1329 in the seafaring Ribera neighbourhood, Santa Maria del Mar was paid for and built by its own parishioners. It's one of the purest examples of Catalan Gothic.

Three stories most visitors miss

1

The stone carriers

The bastaixos, the port's porters, carried stone on their backs from the Montjuïc quarry to the building site. Their figures are on the bronze main doors.

2

Fast for its time

The last section of the vault was placed in 1345, only 16 years after work began, and the first mass was said the following year.

3

Why it's so bare

In 1936, at the start of the Spanish Civil War, the church burned for days and lost its altars and furnishings. The emptiness that's left shows off its slender columns and wide space.

Santa Maria del Mar: common questions

Who built Santa Maria del Mar?

The people of the Ribera neighbourhood: merchants, sailors and the bastaixos, the port porters who carried the stone. It was begun in 1329.

Why is the inside of Santa Maria del Mar so plain?

A fire in 1936, at the start of the Spanish Civil War, destroyed most of its altars and decoration.

Is Santa Maria del Mar the cathedral from the novel?

Yes. Ildefonso Falcones' novel Cathedral of the Sea tells the story of its construction.
