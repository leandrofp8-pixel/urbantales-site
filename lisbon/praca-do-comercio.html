Three stories most visitors miss

1

The palace that vanished

For over two centuries the kings lived in the Ribeira Palace on this spot. The 1755 earthquake, tsunami and fires destroyed it, which is why locals still call the square Terreiro do Paço, the palace yard.

2

A city rebuilt to a plan

The Marquis of Pombal rebuilt the Baixa as a grid of straight streets, with buildings designed to resist earthquakes, and made this square its gateway from the river.

3

The day a king was killed

On 1 February 1908, King Carlos I and his heir, Prince Luís Filipe, were shot dead as their carriage crossed this square.

Praça do Comércio: common questions

Why is Praça do Comércio called Terreiro do Paço?

Because the royal Ribeira Palace stood here until the 1755 earthquake; Terreiro do Paço means palace yard.

Whose statue is in Praça do Comércio?

King José I on horseback, unveiled in 1775, the king who reigned during the 1755 earthquake and the rebuilding.

What happened in Praça do Comércio in 1908?

King Carlos I and Crown Prince Luís Filipe were assassinated here on 1 February 1908.
