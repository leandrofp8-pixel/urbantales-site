Three stories most visitors miss

1

The Moorish citadel

Before 1147 this was the alcáçova, the fortified heart of Muslim Lisbon, until Afonso Henriques took the city with the help of crusaders.

2

A palace on the hill

The kings of Portugal lived here until the early 16th century, when Manuel I moved down to a new palace by the river.

3

Rebuilt in the 20th century

Much of what you see was reconstructed in the 1930s and 40s, after centuries as barracks, prison and arsenal.

São Jorge Castle: common questions

How old is São Jorge Castle?

The hill has been fortified since at least the Iron Age; the walls you see grew from the Moorish citadel and were heavily restored in the 1930s and 40s.

Did kings live in São Jorge Castle?

Yes, it was a royal residence from the 13th century until the early 16th century.

When was Lisbon taken from the Moors?

In 1147, when Afonso Henriques captured the city with the help of crusaders on their way to the Holy Land.
