Three stories most visitors miss

1

A temporary set made permanent

The first version was built for the 1940 Portuguese World Exhibition. It was rebuilt in concrete and stone in 1960, for the 500th anniversary of Henry the Navigator's death.

2

Who's on the prow

Henry the Navigator leads 32 figures of the Age of Discoveries: navigators such as Vasco da Gama, Pedro Álvares Cabral and Magellan, along with cartographers, writers and missionaries.

3

A map under your feet

The giant compass rose and world map in the pavement, a gift from South Africa, mark the routes and dates of the Portuguese voyages.

Monument to the Discoveries: common questions

When was the Monument to the Discoveries built?

A temporary version was made for the 1940 Portuguese World Exhibition; the permanent one was inaugurated in 1960.

Who is on the Monument to the Discoveries?

Henry the Navigator at the prow and 32 figures of the Age of Discoveries behind him, including Vasco da Gama, Pedro Álvares Cabral and Magellan.

What is the map in front of the monument?

A compass rose and world map in the pavement, a gift from South Africa, tracing the Portuguese sea routes.
