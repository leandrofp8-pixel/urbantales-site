Three stories most visitors miss

1

A neighborhood cleared for it

To make room, a medieval neighborhood on the slope of the Capitoline Hill was demolished. The bright white marble from Brescia never matched Rome's warm travertine, which is part of why the monument divided Romans from the start.

2

The Unknown Soldier

In 1921 the body of an unidentified soldier from the First World War was buried at the Altar of the Fatherland, chosen by the mother of a missing soldier from 11 coffins. Two soldiers guard it day and night.

3

A horse big enough for dinner

Before the bronze statue of Victor Emmanuel II was put in place, its sculptors held a dinner inside the horse's belly. The statue is about 12 meters tall.

Vittoriano (Altare della Patria): common questions

What is the Vittoriano?

A national monument to Victor Emmanuel II, the first king of unified Italy, which also holds the Altar of the Fatherland and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier.

When was the Vittoriano built?

Construction began in 1885. It was inaugurated in 1911 and finished in 1935.

Can you go to the top of the Vittoriano?

Yes. A glass elevator takes visitors to the terrace on the roof, with a ticket.
