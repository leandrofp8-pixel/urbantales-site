Green flags — good signs on a profile

  • Posts about walking through cities — neighborhoods, streets, landmarks
  • Shares curiosities or history in captions, not just aesthetics
  • Explores independently (not packaged group tours)
  • Comments are questions or reactions — engaged, curious audience
  • Covers multiple cities — shows they travel regularly
  • Storytelling tone — writes about what a place means, not just looks

Red flags — skip these

  • Mostly beach, resort, or luxury hotel content — not our audience
  • Follower count huge but comments are just emojis — fake or ghost followers
  • Every post is sponsored — they only work for pay, not fit
  • Covers only one city or region — audience isn't travel-minded
  • Gear/packing/flight deals content — logistics focus, not experience
  • "DM for collabs" in bio — agency managed, expect paid-only replies

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Personalize every message. Mention one specific thing you saw on their profile — a video, a city they covered, their style. Generic messages get ignored.
  • Match the language. If they post in Portuguese, write in Portuguese. Always.
  • Lead with the invite, not the deal. Ask them to try the app first. Mention collaboration only as a secondary option.
  • Keep it short. 4–5 short paragraphs max. People don't read long DMs from strangers.
  • Follow up once after 2–3 weeks of silence. Use the follow-up template. Then move on.
  • Sign as Leo. First person from the founder feels warmer than a brand message.

✗ Don't

  • Don't open with "partnership" or "collaboration". It immediately reads as a paid pitch and gets ignored or declined.
  • Don't mention payment or commission rates in the first message. That comes only if they show interest.
  • Don't send the same message to everyone. Even a small detail makes a huge difference in response rate.
  • Don't follow up more than once. Two messages total. Anything more is spam.
  • Don't target mega-influencers (1M+). Almost zero response rate and poor conversion even if they do post.
  • Don't pitch influencers whose content doesn't fit. If they only post beaches and resorts, skip them.
