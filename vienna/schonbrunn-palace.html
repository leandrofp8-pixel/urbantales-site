Three stories most visitors miss

1

The beautiful spring

Emperor Matthias is said to have found a spring here while hunting in 1612 and called it 'schöner Brunnen', the beautiful spring. The source still flows inside a small pavilion in the gardens.

2

Mozart, aged six

In 1762 a six-year-old Mozart played for Maria Theresa in the Hall of Mirrors and, the story goes, jumped into the empress's lap and kissed her.

3

The oldest zoo still open

The Tiergarten Schönbrunn was founded in 1752 as an imperial menagerie, which makes it the oldest zoo in the world that's still open.

Schönbrunn Palace: common questions

Why is it called Schönbrunn?

After a 'beautiful spring' (schöner Brunnen) Emperor Matthias is said to have found here in 1612.

How many rooms does Schönbrunn have?

1,441 rooms; around 40 are open to visitors.

Was the Austrian State Treaty signed at Schönbrunn?

No, it was signed at the Belvedere in 1955. Schönbrunn hosted the Congress of Vienna's diplomats and the 1961 Kennedy–Khrushchev summit dinner.
