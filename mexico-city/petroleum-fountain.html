Three stories most visitors miss

1

The day Mexico took its oil back

On 18 March 1938, President Lázaro Cárdenas expropriated the oil industry from foreign companies, mostly American and British.

2

Paid with chickens

Mexicans rushed to help pay the compensation, giving money, jewelry and even chickens. The fountain opened on the 14th anniversary, in 1952.

3

The Diana's makers

Vicente Mendiola and Juan Fernando Olaguíbel, who designed it, were the same pair behind the Diana the Huntress fountain further down Reforma.

TipIt sits in the middle of a busy roundabout, so admire it from the edge, ideally at night when the water and bronze are lit. With Urban Tales running, the fountain's story plays as you reach the Glorieta de Petróleos.

Petroleum Fountain (Fuente de Petróleos): common questions

What does the Fuente de Petróleos commemorate?

The oil expropriation of 18 March 1938 by President Lázaro Cárdenas.

When was the Fuente de Petróleos built?

It was inaugurated on 18 March 1952.

Who designed the Fuente de Petróleos?

Architect Vicente Mendiola with sculptor Juan Fernando Olaguíbel.
