Tomb of the Unknown Soldier: the history, and the changing of the guard

Three stories most visitors miss

1

The fallen warrior

The relief shows a dead warrior in the style of an ancient hoplite, lying on his back with a round shield and an ancient Greek helmet. Fokion Rok carved it between 1930 and 1932.

2

Words from Pericles

Above the figure: "To an unknown soldier." On either side, two lines from Pericles' funeral oration, as recorded by Thucydides: "One empty bier, made up for the unidentified" and "The whole earth is the tomb of famous men."

3

The changing of the guard

The Evzones of the Presidential Guard change every hour, with a slow, high-stepping march. On Sunday mornings at 11 a larger ceremony takes place, with a band.

Tomb of the Unknown Soldier: common questions

When is the changing of the guard in Athens?

Every hour, on the hour, day and night. The larger ceremony with a band takes place on Sunday at 11:00.

Who are the Evzones?

Soldiers of the Presidential Guard, an elite ceremonial unit. Their uniform includes the pleated fustanella kilt and tsarouchia shoes with pompoms.

What do the inscriptions say?

"To an unknown soldier", with two lines from Pericles' funeral oration recorded by Thucydides: "One empty bier, made up for the unidentified" and "The whole earth is the tomb of famous men."
