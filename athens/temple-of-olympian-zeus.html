Three stories most visitors miss

1

Six centuries of stop and start

The sons of Peisistratos began it around 520 BC and abandoned it when their rule fell. The Seleucid king Antiochus IV restarted it in 174 BC, and Hadrian finally completed it in the 2nd century AD.

2

The column on the ground

Fifteen columns still stand. A sixteenth lies in pieces where a storm blew it down in 1852, and you can see how its drums were stacked.

3

Built for a giant statue

Hadrian placed a huge gold-and-ivory statue of Zeus inside, and set up a statue of himself beside it.

Temple of Olympian Zeus: common questions

How long did the Temple of Olympian Zeus take to build?

About 638 years: it was begun around 520 BC and completed under Hadrian in the 2nd century AD.

How many columns are left?

Fifteen are standing, and a sixteenth, blown down in 1852, lies on the ground. The temple originally had 104.

Who was the temple dedicated to?

Zeus, king of the Olympian gods.
