Three stories most visitors miss

1

Not really a cathedral

Despite its name, it has never been the seat of a bishop. It's the Protestant Supreme Parish and Collegiate Church, built as the court church of the Hohenzollern dynasty.

2

The night the dome burned

In May 1944, a fire burned the lantern on top of the dome and it crashed into the church. In East Berlin, restoration only began in 1975, and the church reopened in 1993.

3

Kings beneath the floor

The Hohenzollern Crypt holds around 90 coffins of the dynasty, including Frederick I, the first King of Prussia, and his wife Sophie Charlotte.

Berlin Cathedral: common questions

Is Berlin Cathedral Catholic?

No, it's Protestant, and despite the name it has never been a bishop's seat.

Can you climb the dome of Berlin Cathedral?

Yes, 270 steps lead to a walkway around the dome.

When was Berlin Cathedral built?

Between 1894 and 1905, for Emperor Wilhelm II, designed by Julius Raschdorff.
