Three stories most visitors miss

1

The night of the fires

On 10 May 1933, students and professors, cheered on by Joseph Goebbels, burned books by Heinrich Mann, Erich Kästner, Sigmund Freud, Albert Einstein and many others here.

2

The empty library

Look down through the glass plate in the square: Micha Ullman's memorial is an underground room of empty white shelves, with space for about 20,000 books.

3

Heine's warning

A plaque nearby quotes Heinrich Heine, writing in 1821: "Where they burn books, they will in the end burn people too."

Bebelplatz: common questions

What happened at Bebelplatz?

On 10 May 1933, the Nazis burned around 20,000 books here, among them works by Heinrich Mann, Freud and Einstein.

Where is the book burning memorial?

In the middle of Bebelplatz, under a glass plate set into the cobblestones.

Who designed the memorial?

The Israeli artist Micha Ullman; it was unveiled in 1995.
