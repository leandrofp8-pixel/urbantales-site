How Urban Tales works

The same app works
in every city on earth.

You don't need a different app for each destination. Urban Tales detects where you are and plays the right stories automatically — wherever you travel.

GPS

Stories triggered by location

As you walk close to a landmark, the audio starts automatically. No tapping, no searching. Put your phone in your pocket and just walk and listen.

4

Storytelling styles in every city

Switch between Historical, Legends, Fun Facts and Cinematic at any time. The same landmark sounds completely different in each mode.

∞

No fixed routes or schedules

Walk at your own pace, stop whenever you want, change direction freely. Use the route generator or ignore it entirely and just wander.
